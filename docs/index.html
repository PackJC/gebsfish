How the preference system works

When you cast, the mod builds a weighted pool of every fish eligible for your water and method. Each fish's weight is CatchProbability × weather × time-of-day × temperature × bait — so a worm at dawn in a warm pond makes bluegill dramatically more likely than a shark. Unlisted bait/fish pairs default to a neutral ×1.0, and the whole system can be disabled with Enable: 0 in bait.json without losing your tuning.

Systems

Every system is independently toggleable in the $profile:Gebs/ config files. Disabled systems keep their tuned values in JSON with no in-game effect.

🌦 Weather & Time of Day

Per-species Rain/Storm/Dawn/Day/Dusk/Night multipliers. Bass fire up at dawn and dusk, walleye and catfish wake at night, trout chase the rain. Stacked buffs are capped so a stormy night never runs away.

🌕 Moon Phases

Real synodic moon-phase math (Meeus algorithm) from the in-game date. Full-moon nights bite up to +20%, new-moon nights down to -10%. Night-only, independently toggleable.

🌡 Water Temperature

Every species has an optimal temperature and a survivable range. Bass own hot summer days; trout and salmon take over in the cold. A global WaterTempOffset adapts the whole curve to winter or tropical maps.

🕐 Bite-Speed Cycles

Each fish has a 24-hour circadian activity curve. The active pool's aggregate drives how long you wait between bites — night fishing for catfish feels different from noon panfishing.

🪱 Living Bait

Worms, grubs, crickets and grasshoppers die about 90 minutes after you find them. Only a Worm Container, a Bug Container or a cooler pauses that clock — tackle boxes and the Bait Bucket do not. Artificial lures never die.

🧊 Cooler & Freezer

Coolers actively chill cargo to -5°C: rot stops, food freezes. Frozen fish can't be filleted until thawed.

🐺 Predators

Fishing, filleting, failed casts, and net use can each draw wolves or bears (~1% per action by default, per-action configurable). Land-only spawns, optional warning sound for everyone nearby, and a chat warning for whoever caused it.

🪝 Hook Recovery

Roughly 1 in 250 fillets recovers a damaged hook or lure "stuck in the fish." Pool and damage range are configurable.

🥫 Junk Catches

Rods and nets can pull configurable junk — wellies, pots, anything you add — with weighted chances and spawn-damage ranges.

💰 Treasure

Roughly 1 in 5000 catches pulls up a container full of loot. Real fishing rods only — the improvised rod never rolls — and each haul costs the rod a third of its health. Admins define the containers and contents; every slot rolls separately so no two hauls match. Off with a single toggle.

📊 Admin Logging

Timestamped session logs in $profile:Gebs/logs/. Debug level 1 gives per-cast summaries; level 2 prints full per-fish breakdown tables that answer "why isn't fish X spawning."

Preparing Your Catch

Gutting isn't a craft recipe in the usual sense — combine any knife with a fish to fillet it. What you get depends on the species:

  • Standard fish — 1 to 4 fillets, depending on the species
  • Roe-bearing fish (trout, salmon, sturgeon, pike, muskellunge) — fillets plus a caviar, subject to the configurable caviar chance
  • Lobsters — a tail plus claws
  • Crabs — legs

The gebsfish fish knives (six colours) work like any knife but fillet faster, by a server-configurable multiplier.

18 species can't be gutted at all — the crayfish, minnows, frogs, salamanders, clams, mussels, snails, starfish and jellyfish, plus sardines, shrimp and bitterlings. They're bait and curiosities, not food. Frozen fish can't be filleted either — let a cooler-chilled catch thaw first.

Finding Bait

Two gathering actions, both configurable and both able to come up empty:

  • Dig for worms — use a digging tool on suitable ground. Can also turn up a Grub Worm
  • Dig for bugs — same idea, yields insects. Requires a Bug Container to keep them alive

Both wear the tool and train soft skills on every completed dig, whether or not you find anything.

Full Guide

Everything the mod does, how it fits together, and every gotcha worth knowing before it surprises you. If you only read one part, read Caveats & Gotchas at the bottom. If you want to know exactly why you caught what you caught, read The Odds, In Detail.

1. Getting Started

Gebsfish needs Community Framework (CF) loaded alongside it, on both the server and the client. Nothing else is required.

To catch your first fish you need a rod, a hook, and bait. Assemble the rod, attach a hook, put bait on the hook, then face water and use the rod. Traps and nets are separate paths that need no rod at all.

Vanilla DayZ fishing still works exactly as it did. Gebsfish replaces what comes out of the water and adds systems around it — it does not change how you cast.

2. Catching Fish

The three methods

  • Rod — the main method. Works in ponds, lakes and sea. Every species with rod in its catch methods is available to you.
  • Large trap — passive. Set it and come back. Catches bigger species that rods also see.
  • Small trap — passive, and the only way to get most of the small aquatic life: minnows, crayfish, shrimp, frogs, salamanders, clams and mussels.
  • Bamboo net — its own catch table, separate from the species list, configured under BambooFishingNetSettings.

What actually decides what bites

Every cast builds a weighted pool from the 79 species. A fish has to clear three gates before it can even enter that pool:

  1. Environment — 1 pond/freshwater, 2 sea/saltwater, 3 both. A sea fish will never appear in a pond.
  2. Catch method — a bitmask you add together: 1 rod + 2 large trap + 4 small trap. So 3 = rod and large trap, 7 = everything.
  3. Catch probability — a 0–25 abundance weight. 0 means uncatchable, which is how you retire a species without deleting it.

Once a fish is in the pool, these multiply its odds:

  • Weather — per-species rain and storm multipliers.
  • Time of day — separate dawn / day / dusk / night multipliers.
  • Moon phase — real synodic math from the in-game date. Full moon nights up to +20%, new moon down to −10%. Night only.
  • Water temperature — each species has an optimal temperature and a survivable range. Outside that range it drops to a tenth of its normal odds — heavily suppressed, but never quite zero.
  • Bait — roughly 700 seeded bait/lure-to-fish pairings bias which fish takes the hook.

Bite speed is separate from what you catch — it controls how long you wait. Each species carries a 24-hour activity curve, and the active pool's aggregate sets the delay between bites. Night fishing for catfish genuinely feels different from midday panfishing.

3. The Odds, In Detail

This section is the arithmetic behind every catch — the real formulas, the real percentages, and the reasoning that produces them. Every number here is computed from the shipped default config, so if you have edited fish.json or junk.json your server's numbers will differ. The method is identical either way.

The one thing to understand first

Two completely separate systems are running, and almost every "the multipliers don't work" report comes from conflating them:

QuestionAnswered byUsesRolled
How long until something bites?ModifySignalProbabilityThe global multipliers in WeatherSettings — one set of numbers for the whole serverEvery signal cycle, continuously
What is on the end of the line?GenerateResultThe per-species multipliers in fish.json, plus baitOnce, the moment you cast
The fish is decided when you cast, not when you reel in. The pool is assembled and drawn from at the start of the action, and that result is locked in — the bite timing that follows only determines whether you get it. Nothing you do mid-cast changes which species is waiting.

The weight formula

Every eligible species gets an integer weight. That weight is what the draw actually uses:

// per species, recomputed every cast speciesMul = min( rainOrStorm × timeOfDay × temperature , MaxStackedMultiplier ) weight = round( 1000 × speciesMul × baitMul ) // the pool: each species contributes its weight once per abundance point poolTotal = Σ CatchProbability(i) × weight(i) P(species) = CatchProbability × weight ÷ poolTotal

Three things about this are worth pausing on, because they explain most surprising results:

  • The 1000 is just fixed-point arithmetic. The draw uses the synchronised integer RNG so client and server stay in lockstep, so float multipliers are scaled to integers. It has no effect on the odds.
  • CatchProbability and the multipliers do different jobs. Probability is a whole-number abundance count (0–25); the multipliers are a continuous scale on top. A probability-3 fish with a 2.0× multiplier still loses to a probability-22 fish sitting at 1.0×.
  • Bait is applied after the cap. MaxStackedMultiplier clamps the weather × time × temperature product, then bait multiplies on top of the clamped value. With the default 2.0 cap and a 2.5× bait, one species can reach 5× — bait is the single strongest lever in the system.

Water temperature: the strongest natural filter

Temperature is a piecewise-linear tent per species: 1.0 at TempOptimal, falling away linearly in both directions, and flattening to a floor of 0.1 once you pass TempMin or TempMax.

water ≤ TempMin → 0.1 // MinTempMultiplier water < TempOptimal → lerp( 0.1 → 1.0 ) across TempMin..TempOptimal water = TempOptimal → 1.0 water > TempOptimal → lerp( 1.0 → 0.1 ) across TempOptimal..TempMax water ≥ TempMax → 0.1 // MaxTempMultiplier
Out of range is a 10× penalty, not a ban. The floor is 0.1, not 0 — you can still land a bluegill in near-freezing water, it is just ten times harder. Set MinTempMultiplier / MaxTempMultiplier to 0 yourself if you want a hard seasonal lockout.

Time of day, and the cap that actually bites

The day is cut into four windows. Exactly one applies at any moment — they never overlap or stack with each other.

Rain and storm are mutually exclusive — a storm replaces rain rather than adding to it. What does stack is rain/storm × window × moon, and that product is clamped by MaxStackedMultiplier (default 2.0). Here is every interesting combination, with the raw product and what the game actually uses:

Why you sometimes catch nothing but boots

Junk is registered in the same pool as fish, with the same 0–25 abundance scale — but junk is not a fish, so none of the multipliers touch it. Its weight is a flat 1000 forever. Fish weights move; junk's does not.

fish weight = 1000 × speciesMul × baitMul // 100 … 5000 junk weight = 1000 // always

The consequence is not obvious and it matters: junk's share of your catches is a direct readout of how hostile the water is. When conditions push every fish toward the 0.1 floor, junk keeps its full weight and inherits the pool.

Two practical readings of that curve:

  • Players: a run of boots is information, not bad luck. The water is outside what the local fish tolerate — move to different water, wait for a different season, or fish at a different hour.
  • Admins: turning the temperature system on raises junk rates on cold maps, because nothing scales junk down alongside the fish. On a winter map, either lower the junk entries' CatchProbability or set a positive WaterTempOffset to bring the whole curve back into range.

Bait pushes the other way. Because bait raises fish weights and leaves junk untouched, a well-matched bait suppresses junk as a side effect — the worked example above drops junk from 10.31% to 7.62% purely by adding a worm.

Treasure, decomposed

A treasure hit is a gate plus three nested random choices. First the rod check, then the trigger roll, then the container, how many slots it has, and each slot independently.

0. Right rod? improvised rod → no roll at all // RequireRealRod, before the chance 1. Does it happen? roll ≤ 0.0002 // once per successful catch 2. Which container? weighted pick from TreasureContainers 3. How many slots? random in that container's MinItems..MaxItems 4. What is in it? per slot, weighted pick from TreasureLoot

The gate runs before the trigger roll, so casting with the crafted improvised rod never even burns a chance — the 1-in-5000 only ticks on a proper rod. And the win is paid for in tackle: every successful pull removes maxHealth ÷ RodCatchesToRuin from the rod — a third of its health at the default 3 — so a pristine rod affords exactly three treasures before it's ruined. Step 4 running per slot rather than once is what makes two identical containers hold different things. The shipped containers:

Weights are pure ratios, so doubling one entry doubles its share and everything else re-normalises automatically — you never have to make the column sum to anything. Setting a weight to 0 retires an entry while keeping it in the file, which is what you want, because a deleted row gets re-added on the next version bump.

Reading the numbers off your own server

You do not have to take any of this on faith. DebugLogs: 2 in general.json prints the whole computation to $profile:Gebs/logs/ for every cast:

  • A per-species table with columns for rain/storm, window, time multiplier, temperature multiplier, combined weather multiplier, bait multiplier and final scaled weight — the exact inputs to the formula above.
  • The assembled pool, listing every eligible species with its weight, environment mask, method mask and abundance. This answers "why isn't fish X spawning" directly, because a fish absent from the list failed a gate.
  • Every clamp, logged the moment it happens, so a multiplier that isn't behaving is visible rather than inferred.
  • The draw itself — roll, pool total, and which species won.
Level 2 is genuinely verbose — it writes several dozen lines per cast. Use it to answer a specific question and then turn it back down; leave it on and a busy server will produce enormous logs. Logs older than 3 days are pruned automatically, which limits the damage but does not eliminate it.

4. Tools & Gear — what each one is for

The Items & Gear tab lists everything the mod adds. This section is about what each piece actually does for you, and when it's worth the inventory space.

🎣 Fishing rods

Four coloured rods on top of the vanilla one. They're cosmetic in performance but wear on every catch outcome — success or failure alike, so a long unlucky session costs the same durability as a good one. A rod can be repaired back up to Worn, never to pristine.

🔪 Fish knives

Six colours. Any knife fillets a fish, but the gebsfish knives are the ones worth carrying: about 54% more durability than a vanilla hunting knife and 10% faster filleting (server-configurable via FishKnifeSpeedMultiplier). If you fillet in bulk, this is the single biggest quality-of-life item in the mod.

🧰 Fishing Rod Repair Kit

Combine with a damaged rod to restore it. The kit is not consumed — it takes durability itself and can be reused until it wears out, so one kit covers many repairs.

🕸 Bamboo Fishing Net

Craft from a Long Wooden Stick + Netting; repair with more Netting. The net is a passive catch method with its own table, separate from the 79-species list — it pulls minnows, frogs and salamanders rather than the rod pool. Catches drop into its own 4×4 cargo, falling back to your feet if that fills up.

🪤 Traps

Large and small traps are the passive alternative to casting. The small trap is the only way to get most small aquatic life — minnows, crayfish, shrimp, frogs, salamanders, clams and mussels. If you want live bait or a Bait Bucket stocked, this is how.

🧊 Coolers

Twelve colours. A cooler actively chills its cargo toward −5 °C: rot stops, live bait stops dying, and food freezes. It's the only container that preserves everything — but see the caveat below.

A cooler will freeze your catch, and frozen fish can't be filleted. That's the trade-off for perfect preservation — let it thaw before you gut it.

🪣 Bait containers

Three containers, three different jobs, and they do not overlap — see the table in the next section. The short version: Worm and Bug Containers keep live bait alive, the Bait Bucket keeps aquatic catches from rotting, and tackle boxes preserve nothing at all.

🧰 Tackle boxes

Small, Medium, Large and Fun variants across many colours. Pure storage — they hold lures, bait, tools, and the Large boxes hold knives too. They preserve nothing, which is exactly what makes the dedicated containers worth carrying alongside them. Large and Medium boxes are also deck-mountable on the jon boat.

🏆 Wooden Fish Mount

The trophy plaque — crafted, wall-mounted, and permanent once a fish is on it. See Trophy Mounts below.

👕 Angler clothing

Fishing hats, shirts and gloves in several colours. Cosmetic — they carry no fishing bonus.

5. Bait & Containers

Live bait — worms, grubs, crickets, grasshoppers — dies about 90 minutes after you find it. Artificial lures never perish. Which container you use is the whole game here, and the containers do not overlap:

6. Handling Your Catch

Gut a fish with any knife to get fillets. Some species also yield a bonus: caviar from roe-bearing fish, tails and claws from lobsters and crabs. Fillets cook like any vanilla meat.

18 species cannot be gutted at all — by design, not a bug. They are bait and curiosities, not food:
Bitterlings, Sardines, Shrimp, American Bull Frog, Black Devil Snail, Blood Clam, Blue Jellyfish, Cave Crayfish, European Crayfish, Fathead Minnow, Florida Crayfish, Monongahela Crayfish, Mussel, Red Salamander, Red Swamp Crayfish, Rusty Crayfish, Signal Crayfish, Starfish.

Frozen fish cannot be filleted. If you have been carrying a catch in a cooler it will freeze — let it thaw before you try to gut it.

Filleting has two side effects worth knowing: it can draw a predator (see below), and roughly 1 in 250 fillets recovers a damaged hook or lure "stuck in the fish."

Predators

Fishing, filleting, a failed cast, and net use can each attract wolves or bears — about 1% per action by default, configurable per action. Spawns are land-only, so no underwater wolves. Everyone nearby may hear a warning sound; the player who caused it also gets a chat warning.

7. Treasure

Very occasionally a catch brings up something other than a fish: a container, full of loot.

  • Roughly 1 in 5000 catches by default. That's catches, not casts — failed casts cost you nothing. A session that lands 60 fish has about a 1.2% chance of seeing one
  • Only on a real fishing rod. The crafted improvised rod is a stick and a length of rope — it never even rolls. The vanilla rod and all four gebsfish colour rods qualify
  • It wrecks your rod. Winching a loaded container up costs the rod a third of its maximum health — three treasures ruin a pristine rod, and one already worn from ordinary fishing gives out sooner. Bring a repair kit or a spare
  • The container appears at your feet, since they're generally too big for a pocket, and a message tells you it happened
  • Contents differ every time. Each slot in the container rolls the loot pool independently, so two identical chests won't hold the same things

Server owners define both halves in general.json. TreasureContainers lists what the treasure can arrive as — each entry with its own weight, health range and item count, so a rarer container can be made to hold more. TreasureLoot lists what can be inside, each with a weight, health range and quantity range for stackables. Set any entry's weight to 0 to retire it without deleting it.

It ships seeded with plain vanilla classnames so it works immediately, but the pools are meant to be replaced — this is the one feature where the whole point is that you decide what's worth finding.

Turn it off entirely with TreasureSettings.Enable: 0, or by setting Chance to 0. Tune the rarity with Chance — 0.0005 is about 1 in 2000, 0.00005 about 1 in 20000. RequireRealRod: 0 lets the improvised rod find treasure too, and RodCatchesToRuin sets how many pulls ruin a pristine rod (0 = no rod wear).

For the full decomposition — the container odds, expected item counts, per-item chances and how long a first find actually takes — see Treasure, decomposed.

8. Trophy Mounts

The Wooden Fish Mount is a plaque you hang on a wall and display a real catch on.

  • Craft it from 1 Wooden Plank + 1 Metal Wire, with a Hacksaw on you. Combine the plank and wire; the saw is not consumed but does take durability, and the craft is refused without one.
  • Place it by holding to place, then aiming at a wall — it snaps flat and hangs face-out like a picture. Aim somewhere without a wall and it falls back to normal ground placement.
  • The trophy is the actual fish you caught, not a generic model. Its weight and quality persist, so a personal best stays a personal best.
  • Mounted fish never rot. Decay is paused entirely while the fish is on the plaque.
Mounting is permanent. Once a fish is on the plaque you cannot detach it, drag it out, or take it to hands. Destroying the mount does not give the fish back: the trophy is destroyed along with the plaque. Mounting a catch is a one-way decision — otherwise the plaque would be a free never-rots fish locker.

Ruined and rotten fish are refused up front. And note the two separate timers: the fish never rots, but the plaque itself lasts 45 days untouched, the same as a tent or barrel.

9. The Jon Boat

A drivable flat-bottomed boat in five variants — green aluminum, gray aluminum, and desert, snow and forest camo. It has its own damage zones (chassis, engine, three floaters), a spark plug slot, and cargo space.

Deck mounts: two deck slots each take a cooler or a tackle box and show it sitting on the deck. Both slots accept the same families, so run two coolers, two boxes, or one of each.

The vanilla jerry can is deliberately not deck-mountable. A deck slot gives one fixed position and rotation to everything attached to it, and the jerry can's model axes do not match the gebsfish containers — no single orientation suits both. More boat content is coming in a future update.

10. Server Admin Guide

The four config files

Everything lives in $profile:Gebs/. Each file documents its own fields in built-in ...Info strings, so the file is its own reference:

  • general.json — global settings, weather, moon, temperature, predators, recipe toggles, hook recovery, per-action net/dig tables
  • fish.json — all 79 species, one row each
  • bait.json — the bait/lure preference matrix
  • junk.json — junk catch tables

How config updates work

This decides which of your edits survive a mod update, so it is worth understanding once:

  • Each file carries its own ConfigVersion. A file is only rewritten when something actually changed — a fresh generation, a re-seeded section, or a version bump. An up-to-date file is left completely alone.
  • A version bump rewrites all four files, even if only one gained anything. Your values are carried through untouched: the file is re-serialized from what was loaded, not regenerated from defaults.
  • Your values are never overwritten. Updates are strictly additive — missing default entries get inserted, existing entries are left exactly as you set them.
  • Deleting an entry does not disable it. A row you remove counts as missing and gets re-added on the next version bump. Set its weight / chance / CatchProbability to 0 instead — that survives every update.

Economy files

On server start the mod writes gebsfish-types.xml and gebsfish-spawnabletypes.xml into $profile:Gebs/mpmissions/. You must merge these into your mission's economy files yourself — the mod cannot do it for you. They contain only Gebsfish classnames; vanilla fish are already in your mission's own types.xml.

Logging and debugging

Timestamped session logs go to $profile:Gebs/logs/. DebugLogs: 1 gives per-cast summaries; DebugLogs: 2 prints full per-fish breakdown tables that answer "why isn't fish X spawning." Logs older than 3 days are deleted automatically at startup so an unattended server does not accumulate one file per restart.

11. Caveats & Gotchas

Everything below is intended behaviour that commonly reads as a bug.

For players

Some things can't be gutted

18 species are catch-only — all the crayfish, minnows, frogs, salamanders, clams, mussels, snails, starfish and jellyfish, plus sardines, shrimp and bitterlings. They are bait, not food.

Frozen fish won't fillet

Cooler-chilled fish freeze. Let it thaw first.

Tackle boxes preserve nothing

They carry bait but never keep it alive. That is what makes the dedicated containers worth the space.

The Bait Bucket isn't for bait

Despite the name, it holds small aquatic catches and refuses worms and insects outright.

Mounting a fish is permanent

No detaching, ever. Destroying the mount is the only way to get the fish back.

The plaque still expires

The mounted fish never rots, but the mount itself has a 45-day untouched lifetime like a tent.

Fishing can get you killed

Casting, filleting, failed casts and nets can all draw wolves or bears.

Deck slots are picky

Jon boat deck mounts take coolers and tackle boxes only — not jerry cans.

For server admins

Never delete config rows to disable something. Deleted entries are treated as missing and get re-added on the next version bump. Set weight, chance or CatchProbability to 0 instead.
BiteSpeed must be a space-separated string of 24 hourly values ("0.85 0.9 1 …"), not a JSON array. DayZ's JSON loader crashes on nested float arrays.
Keep every config string under about 900 characters. The engine's reader hard-crashes past roughly 1024, and the limit applies to the built-in default text too, not just your edits.
  • CF is required on both sides. Server-only installs will not work.
  • A version bump rewrites all four config files, not just the one that changed. Values are preserved — the timestamps changing is expected, not data loss.
  • Economy XML is not merged for you. The generated types must be copied into your mission or nothing spawns.
  • There is no migration from fishingsettings.json. Upgrading from pre-3.3 archives the old layout into $profile:Gebs/gebs_oldfiles/ for reference and generates fresh defaults — re-apply your tuning by hand.
  • Fish quality defaults to 1.0 deliberately, because DayZ-Expansion-Market, TraderPlus and Dr. Jones only accept full-quality items. Lower FishQuality only if your trader scales price by quality.
  • Logs older than 3 days are deleted at startup. Archive anything you want to keep.
  • Disabled systems keep their tuned values in JSON with no in-game effect, so toggling one off never loses your numbers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to run Gebsfish?
Gebsfish plus Community Framework (CF), loaded on both server and client. That's it — everything else is optional tuning.
Where are the config files?
In your server profile folder: $profile:Gebs/general.json (global settings, weather, predators), fish.json (all 79 species), bait.json (the bait preference matrix), and junk.json (junk catches). Delete any of them and the server regenerates fresh defaults on next start. Every file documents its own fields in built-in ...Info strings.
I'm upgrading from an older version — where did fishingsettings.json go?
The old single-file config was replaced by the four files above. It's a clean break: there is no automatic migration, so the server generates fresh defaults and you re-apply your custom tuning by hand. Keep a backup of your old file for reference.
Why isn't fish X spawning?
Three usual suspects: its Environment doesn't match where you're fishing (1 = pond, 2 = sea, 3 = both), its CatchMethod doesn't include your method (1 = rod, 2 = large trap, 4 = small trap, added together), or its CatchProbability is 0. Set DebugLogs: 2 in general.json and the server log will print the full pool for every cast — including exactly why each fish was or wasn't eligible.
What are the actual odds of catching a specific fish?
In fresh water with a rod, the pool is 40 species with a combined weight of 408, plus 25 for junk — so an average-abundance fish sits somewhere between 0.7% and 5.8% per catch at baseline. Salt water has a smaller pool (28 species, weight 276), which makes every individual sea species proportionally more likely.

Those baseline numbers then move a lot: weather, time of day, water temperature and bait all multiply a species' weight, and a well-matched bait on a good day can take one fish from 5% to nearly 18%. The Odds, In Detail in the Full Guide has every formula, the real percentages and a full worked example with charts.
Why am I only catching boots?
Because junk is the one thing in the pool that no multiplier touches. Fish weights scale up and down with conditions; junk's weight is fixed forever. So when the water is far outside what the local fish tolerate, every fish drops toward a tenth of its normal weight while junk keeps all of its — and junk's share climbs from its 5.8% baseline to as much as 39% in near-freezing water.

A run of boots is therefore information, not bad luck: the water is wrong for the fish that live there. Move to different water, fish a different hour, or wait for the season to turn. Adding well-matched bait also helps directly, because raising fish weights pushes junk's share back down. See Why you sometimes catch nothing but boots for the curve.
Can I turn off the weather / moon / temperature / bait systems?
Yes, each independently. WeatherCatchBoostEnable, MoonPhaseEnable, TemperatureEffectEnable, and BiteSpeedEnable live in general.json under WeatherSettings; the bait system's master toggle is Enable at the top of bait.json. Your tuned values stay in the file while a system is off.
I edited BiteSpeed and my server crashed on startup. What happened?
BiteSpeed must be a single space-separated string of 24 hourly values ("0.85 0.9 1 ..."), not a JSON array — DayZ's JSON loader crashes on nested float arrays. Also keep any single string value in the configs under ~900 characters; the engine's reader hard-crashes past ~1024.
Do the fish work with trader mods?
Yes. Fish quality defaults to 1.0 (full) specifically because popular traders (DayZ-Expansion-Market, TraderPlus, Dr. Jones) only accept full-quality items. If your trader scales price by quality, you can lower FishQuality in general.json.
How do I add the fish to my server's economy?
The mod generates gebsfish-types.xml and gebsfish-spawnabletypes.xml into $profile:Gebs/mpmissions/ on server start — merge those into your mission's economy files. The generated types contain only Gebsfish classnames; vanilla fish are already in your mission's own types.xml.
What is the treasure catch, and how rare is it really?
A very rare chance on a successful catch to pull up a container full of loot instead of a fish. Default is 0.0002 — about 1 in 5000 catches. Note that is catches, not casts, so failed casts cost you nothing: a session that lands 60 fish has roughly a 1.2% chance of seeing one, and a few hundred fish still very probably turns up nothing. That is deliberate — it is meant to be a story someone tells, not something you can farm.

Two catches: it only bites on a real fishing rod — the crafted improvised rod never rolls — and winching a loaded container up costs the rod a third of its maximum health, so three treasures ruin a pristine rod. Admins can relax both (RequireRealRod, RodCatchesToRuin).

The container appears at your feet (they are usually too big to pocket) and its contents are rolled fresh every time, so two identical chests will not hold the same things.
How do I change what treasure contains, or turn it off?
Two pools in general.json, both entirely yours to define:

TreasureContainers — what the treasure arrives as. Each entry has a Weight, a health range, and its own MinItems/MaxItems, so a rarer container can be made to hold more.
TreasureLoot — what can be inside. Each entry has a Weight, a health range, and a MinQuantity/MaxQuantity for stackables like ammo or nails.

Every item slot rolls the loot pool independently. Any classname on your server works, including ones from other mods. Set an entry's Weight to 0 to retire it rather than deleting it — deleted rows come back on the next version bump.

It ships seeded with plain vanilla items so it works immediately; those are placeholders and meant to be replaced. To switch it off entirely set TreasureSettings.Enable to 0 (or Chance to 0). To retune, 0.0005 is about 1 in 2000 and 0.00005 about 1 in 20000.
How do I keep my bait alive?
Live bait (worms, crickets, grasshoppers, grubs) dies about 90 minutes after you find it. Store it in a Worm Container, Bug Container or a cooler to pause that clock. The rubber worm is artificial and never dies.

The Bait Bucket is a different job: it holds small aquatic catches — minnows, crayfish, shrimp, frogs and salamanders — and stops them rotting. It will not accept worms or insects, and it is not where you keep live bait alive. Tackle boxes can carry bait but preserve nothing, which is what makes the dedicated containers worth the space.
Why did my rod break? Can I repair it?
Rods and hooks wear a little on every catch outcome — success or fail. Repair rods with the Fishing Rod Repair Kit and the bamboo net with Netting, but repairs cap at Worn and Ruined tools cannot be repaired, so retire gear before it's too late.
Can I turn predators off (or up)?
Fully configurable in general.json: a per-action chance for fishing, filleting, failed casts, and net use (default ~1% each), the predator list with weights and counts, spawn radius, the warning sound, and the chat warning. Set the chances to 0 to disable, or crank them for a high-stakes server.
Does Gebsfish work on custom maps?
Yes — Chernarus, Livonia, Sakhal, Banov, Namalsk, Deer Isle, Lux, NavalPlus, Sahrani, Artseinen, MelkartV2, and Deadfall are supported out of the box. If your map's fishing doesn't work, open a ticket in the Discord and we'll issue a hotfix for that map.
Can I put Gebsfish in my server pack / mod pack?
No. Repacking is not permitted under any circumstances — use a Steam Collection to include the mod on your server. See the Usage & Terms.
Can I monetize my server while running Gebsfish?
Yes, provided you follow the DayZ Server Monetization agreement and have permission from Bohemia.

Enforce exposes no flush, so CloseFile is the only thing that commits bytes to disk — opening and closing around each write means a hard crash can't take the tail of the log with it, and the file stays unlocked so it can be read or moved while the server is running

Age is read from the filename (the generator owns the YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS_tag.log format) because Enforce's file API exposes no modification time; anything that doesn't parse as that format is left untouched, and the live session file is never a candidate
  • Old-layout migration now removes the folders it empties. After sweeping fishingsettings.json, Fish/Logs/ and extras/mpmissions/ into gebs_oldfiles, the emptied Fish/ and extras/ trees are deleted deepest-first. Enforce has no remove-directory call, so this depends on the engine accepting an empty directory — each folder is confirmed empty before the attempt, and any that survive are logged by path for manual cleanup instead of a blanket "delete these yourself" message
  • Craft and repair recipes (bamboo net, fishing pole, hook from wire) now register before the data-driven fish loop. RegisterRecipe hands out sequential IDs and actions send that ID over the network, so anything registered after a variable-length loop shifted by however many species that side happened to register
  • In-hands IK registrations collapsed to three name-array loops

  • Vanilla-fish fillet recipes (Carp / SteelheadTrout / Mackerel / WalleyePollock) collapsed from four near-identical ~40-line Init bodies into one shared SetupVanillaFilletRecipe helper on PrepareFish — they now get the same MeatMin/MeatMax inversion guard, MAXIMUM_RESULTS clamp, and caviar-chance logic as every other fish; no-op CanDo/Do overrides deleted

Fixed

  • Fish catches and modded-fish filleting were both broken. Vanilla's YieldItemBase and RecipeBase constructors call Init() themselves, so Init() ran before the per-species row could be handed over and bailed on its null guard — and registration never runs it again. Every fish yield registered with an empty type and zero environment/method masks, and since the yield bank keys on the type hash, all 79 species collapsed onto one empty entry matching nothing: junk was the only possible catch. The data-driven fillet recipes likewise registered with no ingredients and no results, so no modded fish offered a Gut action (the four vanilla fish kept working through their own Prepare* overrides). Setup now applies from the setter instead of relying on constructor-time Init()
  • Clients registered zero fillet recipes: the config left the Species table null on clients until the ConfigSync RPC, which lands after PluginRecipesManager has already registered. Clients now seed the compiled defaults in memory, and the RPC still overwrites them with the server's file
  • Jon boat deck attachments were invisible — the model carried the proxies and the config declared the slots, but nothing tied the two together. Added the ProxyAttachment entries that bind each proxy to its slot. They must live in CfgNonAIVehicles; in cfgVehicles the base class resolves to a new empty one and the malformed entries break the boat's crew config, locking players out of both seats
  • Wooden Fish Mount placement hologram rendered as the normal textured plaque instead of the white ghost: placing was listed in hiddenSelections[] but never declared as a section in Model.cfg, so the hologram material had no swappable selection to land on. The deployable/undeployable materials also needed vanilla's Super shader and full stage chain — flat Normal/Basic with no stages loses the fresnel sheen that makes a hologram read as one
  • Wooden Fish Mount couldn't be placed on walls despite the wall-snapping logic working: EvaluateCollision rejected it through IsFloating, IsBaseViable, IsClippingRoof and HeightPlacementCheck, all of which assume ground placement, and yawPitchRollLimit capped pitch at 89 degrees — one short of flat against a vertical wall. Player collision, permitted-area, underwater and in-terrain checks still apply
  • Jon boat no longer plays the vanilla rubber boat's engine shutdown sound when the engine cuts, including when stepping out of the driver seat. BoatScript hardcodes Boat_01 soundsets for every boat, so the jon boat now has its own script class that suppresses it
  • XML generator crash at startup: FPrint without newlines produced a single-line 85 KB file that overflowed the engine's line-read buffer as fish were added — generators now emit proper line breaks
  • Bait preferences and the temperature curve now apply independently of WeatherCatchBoostEnable — previously the weighted catch pick only ran when the weather toggle was on, silently disabling both systems despite their own toggles
  • BiteSpeed aggregate no longer applies CatchProbability twice (the probability pool already repeats each fish by its weight) — abundant fish were quadratically dominating the bite-cycle timing over rare ones
  • Rods no longer take double durability damage per catch outcome — a leftover duplicate AddHealth call made rods wear at 2x the intended 1.5 HP
  • Hardened edge-case null handling: junk-yield registration now logs and skips (instead of crashing at mission init) when the config failed to load; cooler tick, hook-crafting check, rod-repair plugin lookups, and the debug yield dump all guard references that could be null in broken states

  • Predator warning chat message sends once through the first enabled color instead of once per enabled color
